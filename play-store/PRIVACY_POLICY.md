# NeighborHub Privacy Policy
Last updated: 18 September 2026

Use this text on the webpage you will host, then paste that page URL into Play Console (App content → Privacy policy) and into `lib/core/constants/app_strings.dart` as `privacyPolicyUrl`.

## About NeighborHub
NeighborHub (package `com.neighborhub1.app`) helps residents view water timings, shuttle schedules, official community updates, emergency contacts, and nearby hospital directions.

## Information we collect
- **Location (optional):** used only to estimate distance to nearby hospitals on the map. If you deny permission, the app uses a default community location. We do not track location in the background.
- **Internet access:** used to display Google Maps and to open the phone dialer.

## Information we do not collect
NeighborHub does not create user accounts. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your location for advertising or analytics profiles.

## How information is used
Community phone numbers and schedules shown in the app are local service information. Location is processed on your device to calculate distance. Map tiles are provided by Google Maps under Google’s terms.

## Sharing
We do not share your location or personal information with third parties for advertising. Google Maps may receive map requests when you open the hospital map.

## Your choices
You can deny location permission in Android settings and continue using water timings, shuttle schedules, contacts, and updates.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use Help & Support inside NeighborHub.
