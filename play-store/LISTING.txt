Full description:
NeighborHub is a community information app for residents. Check water plant timings, shuttle service schedules, official neighborhood updates, and essential emergency contacts without hunting through notices or group chats.

What you can do:
• See today’s water distribution hours and the weekly plant schedule
• View shuttle routes and departure times
• Call fire, medical, police, and on-site maintenance numbers quickly
• Read official updates about maintenance, parking, and community events
• Find the nearest hospital on the map (optional location permission)

Location is used only to estimate distance to nearby hospitals. You can deny location access and still use water timings, shuttle info, contacts, and updates.

NeighborHub is built for everyday community services — information at a glance.
